**Rate**: a courier's price quote for sending one order to its Ship To address.
**Ship To**: the delivery address on an order.
**Label**: the printable document, a PDF or a ZPL file, that is attached to the parcel.
**Void**: cancel a shipment before pickup so it is not charged. A voided shipment cannot be reprinted.
**Selected rate**: the rate the seller chose for an order. An order without one cannot be shipped.
**Courier outage**: the courier's service does not answer. ShipNest shows an error and keeps the order unshipped.
